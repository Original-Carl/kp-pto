What Your Contributions Support

Ways to Give

  • Online: when a campaign or event is collecting funds, its Zeffy link is posted on the Events page.
  • Check: make it payable to “KP PTO” and drop it off at the KP Main Office.
  • Cash: put it in an envelope labeled with your student’s name and “KP PTO”, and drop it off at the KP Main Office.
