Our Programs

What King Philip Middle School PTO Inc. funds, organizes, and runs for KP students and staff.

Staff Appreciation

The board funds and arranges annual appreciation lunches for the teachers and staff of King Philip Middle School, and funds and staffs all staff appreciation days.

School Support

The board provides supplementary funds for things like soda crackers in the Nurse’s Office.

Field Trips

The board raises funds to support class field trips and outings. These funds cover transportation, admission, and other associated costs.

Basketball

This year, the board approved funding for boys’ and girls’ basketball teams, open to all students.

Scholarship Fund

The board supports a district-wide scholarship program for exceptional high school students with demonstrated financial need, and contributed to the fund last year.

School Events

The board funds and staffs the Welcome Back Picnic, Movie Nights (The Princess Bride and The Outsiders this year!), the Halloween and Spring Dances, and the 8th Grade Formal Dance. Dates are on the Events page.

PTO Meetings

PTO Board General Meetings are open to the public. Meetings are held on Zoom, and most are also held in the school library; dates are on the Events page.

Volunteering

We are always looking for volunteers! See the Volunteer page to get involved.

How Programs Are Funded

Programs are paid for by contributions, made online through Zeffy or by check or cash at the KP Main Office.
